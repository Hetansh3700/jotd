Agents that know when to shut up

Every proactive agent lives or dies on one property: restraint. The design rule we landed on has two halves. First, the pulse never sends anything to another human — it drafts, it nudges its own user, it prepares, but a person always fires the shot. Second, silence is logged: every candidate the agent considered and chose not to surface gets a written reason in the log, which turns restraint from a claim into an auditable artifact.

The interruption budget does the rest. Hard caps live in code, not prompts, so the model reasons about which loops deserve attention while the runner guarantees how many notifications can ever fire.
